# Review Round 3 — Figures & Tables Quality

You are reviewing a manuscript for Journal of Hydrology.
The manuscript is at: `paper/manuscript.md` in the GitHub repository.

**Your task for Round 3:** Review the figures and tables. Note: you cannot see the actual figures, but you can review the captions and the in-text descriptions. Focus on:
1. **Caption quality** — Are figure captions self-contained? Do they explain what the reader should see? Do they include key experimental conditions?
2. **Table design** — Are tables well-structured? Are column headers clear? Are units specified? Is the information density appropriate?
3. **Figure placement** — Does the order of figures in the text make sense? Is each figure cited before it appears?
4. **Redundancy** — Do any figures and tables duplicate the same information? Can any be merged or removed?
5. **Missing visuals** — Are there results described in the text that would benefit from a figure or table but do not have one?

Provide a structured review with:
- **Major issues** (must fix)
- **Minor issues** (should fix)
- **Suggestions** (optional improvements)

Do NOT edit the manuscript directly. List your findings concisely.
