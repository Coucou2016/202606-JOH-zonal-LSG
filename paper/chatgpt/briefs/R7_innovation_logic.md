# Review Round 1 — Innovation & Logic Audit

You are reviewing a manuscript for submission to Journal of Hydrology.
The manuscript is at: `paper/manuscript.md` in the GitHub repository.

**Your task for Round 1:** Audit the manuscript for:
1. **Novelty claims** — Are they clear, specific, and appropriately scoped? Do they overclaim? Is the contribution clearly distinguished from prior work (Fraehr 2022/2023/2024, Tan 2025, Wang 2025)?
2. **Logical flow** — Is the narrative arc from Introduction → Methods → Results → Discussion → Conclusion coherent? Does each section build on the previous one?
3. **Research questions** — Are the three objectives stated in the Introduction actually answered by the Results and Discussion? Any gaps?
4. **Internal consistency** — Do cross-references (figure numbers, table numbers, section references) make sense? Are there any contradictions?
5. **Missing context** — Is there anything a JOH reviewer would expect to see that is absent?

Provide a structured review with:
- **Major issues** (must fix)
- **Minor issues** (should fix)
- **Suggestions** (optional improvements)

Do NOT edit the manuscript directly. List your findings concisely.
